## Surface
Landing page and project-to-portfolio prototype. Persuade visitors, then let artisans and customers try the sample journey.

## Audience and job
Artisans need a professional home for work currently scattered across chats and camera rolls. Customers need to find relevant local work and contact its maker. Show a project as a record with its story and progress, not as an independently verified credential.

## Direction contract
### THESIS
Each project opens as a readable work record, then joins a portfolio customers can explore. Refuse the generic centered SaaS pitch and engagement-first social feed.

### OWN-WORLD
The chosen project folio: uncoated-paper cream, dark ink, restrained workshop green, and warm timber. Use a characterful editorial face for display, a clear sans for controls, generous image-led layouts, restrained rules, and square-cornered folio details.

### STORY
Move from work scattered across a phone, to one project kept with its brief and milestones, to a portfolio a customer can browse. Visitors can explore sample work or start a sample project.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Keep a compact wordmark and two clear routes in the top navigation. Below, place the large “Let the work tell the story” headline and a short explanation on the left; make one generous photographed project folio the visual lead on the right. Put the browse action first, with portfolio creation beside it. The project record shows its maker, title, completion state, and progress.

### FORM
The project folio, the selected code-led HTML sketch. Its signature interaction is moving a project through planned, in-progress, and completed before a deliberate review and publish action. Direction seed reference: seed:a11fa65266c70149.

### FINISH
unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints
All routes adapt to phone and desktop. Sample records and simulated publish/contact actions must be labeled; no API, persistence, upload, messaging, or privacy enforcement is claimed.
