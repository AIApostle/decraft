# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React + TypeScript, with a mobile-first, portfolio-focused frontend for Decraft. The frontend is a web app that can support artisan portfolios, discovery browsing, and project detail flows.

## Users

Primary users are artisans, independent professionals, workshops, and businesses who need a professional place to document and present their work. Secondary users are customers and clients who need to discover relevant providers near them, review project evidence, and reach out with an inquiry or message.

## Product Purpose

Decraft helps skilled workers turn scattered work history into a structured portfolio that can be searched, reviewed, and contacted. The product makes it easier to document projects over time, keep private and client-shared work separate, and surface work publicly to people looking for a service nearby.

## Positioning

Decraft is a work-centered professional network where the project itself is the core unit of identity. It is differentiated from general social platforms by emphasizing documented work, privacy controls, and practical discovery rather than engagement-first feeds.

## Operating Context

The product is used by people who may be mobile-first, often accessing the platform from phones with uneven connectivity, and working in local service markets where trust and visibility matter. The system must support public portfolio visibility, private project records, client-only links, and the ability to follow a user's project timeline from plan to completion.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities include:
- artisan and business profile creation
- project creation, editing, and organization
- media attachment with project milestones and timeline stages
- AI-assisted drafting for project updates and organization
- explicit approval before public publishing
- public, private, and client-only visibility controls
- location-first discovery and practical filtering
- inquiry and direct messaging flows
- basic moderation and blocking controls

Confirmed constraints include:
- the MVP should remain focused and not include full payments, bidding, or complex admin systems
- AI assistance must support the artisan's decision-making and never auto-publish content
- privacy must be enforced by backend rules, not only UI hiding
- the experience should be optimized for mobile use and resilient to poor connectivity

## Brand Commitments

The product positioning is "Your craft. Your work. Your reputation." The brand is professional, practical, and trust-first rather than entertainment-first.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product requirements in the project PRD define the user roles, MVP scope, and operational constraints.
- Solution approach describes the expected architecture, privacy model, discovery behavior, and general implementation direction.
- Current frontend scaffold is a Vite React app that will be shaped into the Decraft product experience.

## Product Principles

1. Work is the product's primary content.
2. Document once and reuse across portfolio, discovery, and client communication.
3. AI assists the artisan but does not replace approval.
4. Privacy is enforced by product rules and server-side access control.
5. The platform should remain practical, mobile-first, and focused on real-world work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product should be usable on mobile devices, accessible to a broad audience, and designed around real service-work contexts rather than desktop-only assumptions. No product-specific accessibility requirement beyond mobile-first usability and clear inquiry flows was confirmed beyond that.
